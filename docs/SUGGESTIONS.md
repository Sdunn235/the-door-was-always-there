That supports proposing a **pilot with measurement**, which is exactly what the handoff asked for: "illustrate possibility, not claim it's proven."

### N-06 Refutation: an argument outline (write it in your words)
Counterargument: *"Services already exist; men can choose to use them."*
1. **Concede what's true:** yes, the services are real (name the walk-in center and crisis line), and yes, each man decides.
2. **Then the turn:** even with them, about 4 in 5 suicide deaths are men (SRC-01), and residents name cost and waits as barriers (MHCP 2021).
3. **Why choice isn't the whole story:** norms like self-reliance and fear of judgment make the choice harder (SRC-03). A door no one walks through isn't access.
4. **The pilot respects choice:** it's voluntary, and it makes the choice easier and better informed; it doesn't force anyone.
5. **Evidence it can work:** men offered a male-framed online program were more likely to seek professional help (Gilgoff et al., 2023).

### N-07 Memorable close: concept seeds
- **Door callback:** the door was there the whole time; what changed is someone showed him it opens.
- **Number callback:** return to "4 in 5" and turn it into a question for the audience: how many of them walked past a flyer that wasn't written for them?
- **Strength reframe:** asking for help is a strength men already use everywhere else (calling a mechanic, a coach, a teammate).
